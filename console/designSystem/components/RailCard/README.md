One session in the rail. The whole card opens the session; the buttons on it do not.

- Line one: title (`card-title`), an ownership tag when there is one, then star and ⋯. On Everyone, and on Mine for a session you started that someone else has, the Assigned tag is the name of whoever has the session.
- Line two (`meta`): the status word in its color first, so it starts at the same place on every card, then a name only where the card does not already say it: on Mine `from <person>` for a shared session someone else started, on Everyone `from <person>` for one nobody has. In the last 15 minutes before a running session pauses (waiting for input) or stops (at its lifetime), an `amber-ink` `pauses in N min` or `stops in N min` (`timer`) follows; an idle pause never counts down while the agent works. The PR chips or issue number sit at the right end, then Take it, Resume, or in those 15 minutes **Extend** (`btn btn-plain btn-sm`, never orange: it is not the card's primary action), which puts the pause or stop an hour later. Extend is only on a session the viewer has (a shared session's assignee, a private session's owner): anyone else sees the countdown alone. A question from Slack has no Extend. A session with several pull requests has a chip for each, but a merged or closed one only while no newer one came after it, so the newest always shows. At most the newest two show (one on a phone's pane bar); the older fold into a `+N` chip first, in the draft chip's neutral grey (`stopped` ground, `sub` text), that names each on hover. Details lists them all. A PR chip in GitHub's merge queue is `wait` on a 12% wash of `wait`, as GitHub colors it, and says `queued` instead of a mark. While a review or QA session the console started is at work on the pull request, its chip says `review`, `QA` or `review, QA` in the chip's own color and weight 500, as `queued` is, then the spinning ring in place of ✓; a ✗ stays, after the word.
- No running time on the card: it is under the session's Details.
- With more than one project, a 3px left edge in the project's `proj-*` colour (`has-project proj-<key>`), kept through hover and the open session's outline; a row on a phone keeps it too. The project's name is in the card's hover card (`components/RailPeek`), or its tooltip where the pointer cannot hover, and part of its link's accessible name, so the colour is never the only sign. A seventh project, past the palette, has a `line` edge.
- Status colors: `wait` for Needs you and Paused, `slate` for Working and Waiting on a job (a ring, since it waits on a machine), `green` for Done, `bad` for Failed. The word is always shown.
- A stopped session is on `stopped`, title at 12px weight 500, with a ring dot.
- The open session has an `amber` outline.
- The consumer provides the session, its status, the PR and the handlers. No CPU, memory or Start app here; they are in the pane bar.
- **A group** (Mine only): a session started as a piece of another's work (`woodshed-session new`, not `--separate`) sits under it while that one is live (`W.railGroups`), in a `rgroup` indented 10px behind a 2px `line` rule (on a phone, 14px behind a `line-soft` rule, rows flush as everywhere). Groups nest all the way down. The main card's third line is its toggle (`rkids`, `meta` at 10.5px, `sub` ink, no border): `▾ 3 sessions`, then `· 1 needs you` in `wait` weight 600 when any asks its person (a question or approval on screen, or paused: `W.asksPerson`, not a finished one). It folds the group (`▸`), each viewer's choice in their browser (`woodshed-rail-groups`). Inside a group, the sessions asking their person come first, then the live, then the newest. Once the main session stops, its sessions are back on their own, so a live one never hides under a folded Stopped. Its owner moves a session out of its group, or under another running one, from its ⋯ menu (**Move out of its group**, **Put under…**, picked in the console's own choice dialog).
- A session that asked the session that started it a question (`woodshed-session reply --question`) and is done with its turn reads **asked a question** in `wait`, as Needs you does: it is asking its person, through that session.
- A session in a group that asks its person also shows on **Needs you**, as a copy (`is-copy`) whose last line says `In <main session>` (`sub` at 11px, the name a link), so a folded group never hides it.
